1. Introduction & App Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Rage Arcade ("the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), with Android package name com.antigravity.retroragearcade, handles data when installed and operated on Android devices.

The game is developed by Idea AI using the open-source Godot Engine (version 4.7.2). Retro Rage Arcade is a retro arcade platformer featuring its flagship mode, Coin Hell, comprising 20 challenging stages across 4 distinct worlds, concluding with a final dragon boss encounter.

✓ Core Privacy Standard: The developer (Idea AI) does not collect, record, track, transmit, monetize, sell, or rent personal data or personal accounts from players. We operate no remote player databases or tracking servers.

Starting in release v0.14.0 (versionCode 14), the Application integrates third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob to offer players an optional rewarded advertisement feature to revive after in-game death. Sections 5, 6, and 7 below describe this feature, its gameplay rules, and the advertising data processed by Google.

2. Information Handled Directly by Us

Inspection of the application code, build configurations, and compiled release artifacts confirms that Idea AI does not collect or store personal information directly:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for, collect, store, or receive real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, age, gender, or government IDs.
  • No User Accounts or Logins: The game has no account creation, passwords, social media logins (e.g., Google Sign-In, Facebook Login), or cloud player profiles.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: There are no in-app purchases (IAP), subscriptions, paid unlocks, or virtual currency microtransactions. The game is 100% free to play.
  • No First-Party Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track player gameplay sessions, player death events, level run durations, or behavioral telemetry on our own servers.
  • No Direct Identifier Collection: We do not collect or store device hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, or IMEI numbers. (Advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID processed by Google Mobile Ads are handled directly by Google LLC as detailed in Section 6).

3. Locally Stored Game Progress

To preserve gameplay achievements between sessions, the game writes a single JSON save file strictly to your local device storage:

  • Storage File Path: The game writes to Godot's user://retro_rage_save.json path. On Android devices, this resolves to the application's private, sandboxed internal storage directory: /data/data/com.antigravity.retroragearcade/files/retro_rage_save.json.
  • Data Elements Stored:
    1. schema_version: An integer (currently 2) maintaining save file format compatibility across game updates.
    2. highest_unlocked_level: An integer (range 1 to 20) indicating level progression.
    3. completed_levels: An array of integers identifying specific stages cleared by the player.
    4. stage_stars: A dictionary mapping level numbers to star ratings earned (0 to 3 stars).
    5. sfx_enabled: A boolean flag (true / false) saving the sound effects toggle.
    6. music_enabled: A boolean flag (true / false) saving the background music toggle.
    7. level_15_secret_revealed and level_16_secret_revealed: Boolean flags tracking secret stage discoveries.

Strict Local Isolation: This save file exists exclusively on your device hardware. It is never uploaded to any remote server and is never transmitted over any network.

5. Google AdMob Rewarded Advertising

Starting in version 0.14.0, Retro Rage Arcade integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Advertisements in Retro Rage Arcade are implemented strictly as optional rewarded video ads for reviving a fallen character.

5.1 Gameplay Implementation & Revive Mechanics

The rewarded revive ad feature operates under the following precise gameplay rules:

  • Optional Revive: When a player dies in a Coin Hell level, the game displays a Death Overlay. The player may optionally tap 🎬 WATCH AD — REVIVE to view a short rewarded advertisement and revive at the last safe checkpoint foothold.
  • Never Mandatory: Watching an advertisement is completely voluntary. The ↺ RESTART LEVEL button is always enabled and accessible immediately with zero wait time and zero ad playback required.
  • One Revive per Run: A player is permitted a maximum of one (1) rewarded revive per run (enforced by the game's MAX_REVIVES_PER_RUN = 1 logic). If the player dies again after reviving, the revive button is permanently disabled for the remainder of that attempt.
  • Star Rating Scoring Penalty: If a player uses a rewarded revive to complete a level, their maximum star rating for that run is capped at two (2) stars.
  • Three Stars for Clean Runs: If a player completes a level without using a rewarded revive, they remain eligible for a full three (3) star completion.
  • Spawn Protection: When revived, the character receives 1.0 second of bounded invulnerability (with visual sprite flickering) to ensure safe recovery before hazards resume.

5.2 Technical Integration Details

The advertising integration uses the official Godot Android AdMob plugin architecture:

  • Ad SDK Provider: Google Mobile Ads Android SDK (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads:24.9.0) mediated via the native Godot AdMob Android Plugin (version 7.0).
  • Ad Format: Rewarded Video format only. The game does not contain banner advertisements, app open advertisements, native ads, or disruptive unskippable interstitial ads between levels.
  • Ad Verification: Rewards are granted only after the Google AdMob SDK fires an authoritative reward callback confirming complete viewing of the rewarded advertisement. Duplicate and stale callbacks are rejected by the game's transaction management system.

6. Data Processed by Advertising Services

When rewarded advertisements are requested or displayed, Google LLC and its advertising services may automatically collect and process certain device and technical information. Idea AI does not collect, receive, or store this information on developer servers.

Information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (AD_ID / GAID) on Android devices, used for ad serving, frequency capping, ad measurement, and anti-fraud verification.
  • IP Address: Used by Google to determine coarse geographical location (such as country or city) to serve relevant, localized advertisements and detect fraudulent traffic.
  • Device & Hardware Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, system language/locale, and mobile network operator.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad request timestamps, ad load latency, impressions, video viewing completion events, click-through events, and SDK error diagnostic codes (e.g., no fill, network timeout).

Purposes of Advertising Data Processing

Google processes the above data for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. Ad Delivery & Rendering: Loading and displaying video creative assets smoothly on your device.
  2. Reward Verification: Confirming that the player watched the rewarded video ad to validate in-game revive entitlement.
  3. Fraud Prevention & Security: Detecting and combating automated click bots, fraudulent ad traffic, and spoofed requests to protect advertisers and the application.
  4. Ad Personalization & Context: Delivering contextual or personalized advertisements depending on user region, device settings, and Google account preferences.
  5. Aggregated Reporting: Providing the developer with aggregated, anonymized performance metrics (such as total impressions and fill rates) without exposing personal player identities.

7. User Privacy Choices & Advertising Controls

The game does not present an interactive consent dialogue or an in-game settings screen for toggling personalized advertising.

Users who wish to limit advertising tracking, reset their identifiers, or opt out of personalized ads should utilize the device-level and account-level controls described below.

7.2 Operating System Advertising Controls (Android)

Android provides built-in device-level controls that give you direct authority over advertising identifiers and personalized ads:

  • Reset Advertising ID: On Android devices, open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and tap Reset advertising ID. This replaces your current advertising ID with a new random identifier.
  • Delete Advertising ID: On Android 12 and newer, open Settings > Google > Ads and tap Delete advertising ID. When deleted, ad providers cannot access your advertising ID and can only serve non-personalized contextual ads.
  • Privacy Sandbox Controls: On Android 13 and newer, navigate to Settings > Privacy > Ads > Privacy Sandbox to manage topics-based advertising controls and ad measurement settings.

7.3 Google Account Ad Personalization Controls

You can review and manage how Google personalizes advertisements across apps and websites by visiting Google's centralized ad management portal:

  • Google My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com/ — Allows you to customize ad topics or disable personalized ads entirely across your Google account.

7.4 Playing Completely Offline

If you prefer not to interact with advertising networks or share technical device identifiers with Google AdMob, you can disconnect your device from WiFi/cellular data while playing. Retro Rage Arcade functions completely offline: Coin Hell levels, safe checkpoints, collision physics, local saves, and the standard restart mechanics operate without interruption.

9. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because Idea AI does not collect or store user data on external servers, we retain no remote personal data to delete.

How to Delete Your Local Game Progress:

  1. Clear App Data: In Android settings, navigate to Settings > Apps > Retro Rage Arcade > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently removes the local save file (retro_rage_save.json) from internal storage and resets progress to level 1.
  2. Uninstall the Game: Uninstalling Retro Rage Arcade removes the application package and completely deletes its private internal storage sandbox and save files.

Data retained by third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) is managed according to Google's data retention policies, which retain aggregated measurement and anti-fraud diagnostic logs for defined operational periods as detailed in Google's privacy disclosures.
